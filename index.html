Support & FAQ

Does the M3TA Holster fit with my specific red dot optic?
Yes. Our holsters are cut aggressively to accommodate 99% of modern slide-mounted optics (RMR, Holosun, SRO, etc.) without sacrificing structural integrity.
Is there a left-handed version available?
Yes, all our primary platforms are engineered natively in true left-handed configurations. Simply select your dominant hand on the product customization screen.
What is the current lead time for shipping?
All in-stock Quick Ship items leave our facility within 24-48 hours. Custom molded configurations currently have a lead time of 7-10 business days as they are built strictly to order.
Does the Cadre Carrier fit double-stack magazines?
The Cadre Carrier is completely modular. By adjusting the retention screws, it seamlessly scales to securely hold both single-stack and double-stack 9mm/.40 magazines.
Can I attach aftermarket clips to the M3TA Holster?
Absolutely. The chassis is drilled for universal compatibility, allowing you to mount Monoblocks, standard FOMI clips, or soft loops depending on your mission requirements.
What is your return policy?
We run a 30-Day Zero-Risk trial. If the gear doesn't perform to your exact standards, send it back for a full refund. We also back everything with an unconditional lifetime warranty.
